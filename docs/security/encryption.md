# Encryption

## In Transit

All external traffic uses TLS 1.2 or higher. Internal traffic between
services uses mTLS where supported by the host.

## At Rest

Postgres: encrypted volumes at the provider level.
Mongo: encrypted volumes at the provider level.
Redis: encrypted at the provider level. Ledger instance enables TLS.

## Field Level

The following fields are encrypted with KMS-held keys before write:

    national_id
    identity_document_blob
    voice_fingerprint
    dispute_evidence

Keys are rotated annually. Old ciphertext is re-wrapped in place during
rotation windows.

## Application Level

JWT signing uses HS256 with a secret of at least 32 bytes. The secret
is generated by the hosting platform or by scripts/setup.sh.
