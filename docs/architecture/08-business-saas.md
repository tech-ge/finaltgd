# Business SaaS

## Purpose

Give businesses a storefront, payment acceptance, and analytics.

## Storefront

Each business account has a storefront with a catalog. Customers browse
and pay in TGD. Payment uses dynamic QR codes with sub-second clearance.

## Analytics

The dashboard exposes:

    Foot traffic heat maps from geo-located events
    Inventory forecasts from historical sales
    Geo-fenced marketing campaign reach

## Settlement

Cross-border settlement uses TGD internally, removing FX friction for
small businesses. Payout to fiat is a future capability requiring
separate regulatory review.

## Data Isolation

Each business sees only its own data. Analytics are aggregated and
anonymized before crossing tenant boundaries.
